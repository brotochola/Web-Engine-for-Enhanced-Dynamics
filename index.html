WeedJS · Web Engine for Enhanced Dynamics

The multithreaded 2D game engine for the web

Box2D physics, LiquidFun fluids and millions of GPU particles, every system on its own thread. All of it in a browser tab, in about 1 MB.

01 · Fluids

Water that pushes back

LiquidFun, rewritten in C and parallelized to run next to Box2D in WebAssembly. Thousands of particles fall, splash and pool, and carry crates and logs, and the crates push the water too: the coupling goes both ways, every step.

  • two-way coupling
  • WebAssembly + SIMD
  • WGSL water shader
click to drop a crate · drag to stir the lake
02 · Soft bodies

Squishy, and you can cut it

Elastic particle groups hold their shape by matching it again every step. Slice one in two and each half rebuilds its own rest shape and keeps wobbling, as a body of its own.

  • elastic particle groups
  • runtime cutting
  • shape matching
swipe through a jelly to slice it
03 · Joints & light

Hang anything, cut everything

Box2D 3.2 joints in multithreaded WebAssembly: ropes, bridges, hinges, welds that break. The lanterns are real lights: their rays are cast against every body and the shadows swing with them.

  • Box2D 3.2 joints
  • ray-cast lighting
  • soft shadows
swipe across a rope to cut it
04 · Particles & fire

Millions of particles, on the GPU

GPU particles are written once and moved by the vertex shader: no CPU cost per frame, millions alive. Fire is a fluid simulation in WebGPU compute shaders, fed by the bodies that burn.

  • GPU particles
  • WebGPU compute
  • flashes & shadows
click to blow it all up
05 · Under the hood

Every system on its own thread

Physics, logic, spatial queries, particles, render prep, rendering and audio each run in their own worker and share memory through SharedArrayBuffer: no copies, no main-thread stalls. The hottest loops are hand-written C compiled to WebAssembly.

50,000+entities at 60 fps
250,000sprites (bunnymark)
